Classify the interview question into exactly one question_type: behavioral, knowledge, motivation, resume_based, or case. Behavioral asks for a past personal example. Resume_based asks about the candidate's resume, background, projects, strengths, or experience without necessarily asking for a specific past situation. Case asks the candidate to analyze a hypothetical product, business, estimation, strategy, or design problem. Return only the structured schema.
